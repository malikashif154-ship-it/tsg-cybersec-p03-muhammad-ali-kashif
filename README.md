# Social Media Privacy and Account Hardening Audit

This repository contains my submission for **The Sky Gen Cybersecurity Project 03 - Beginner Level**. The project reviews the privacy and security settings of four personal accounts and documents practical steps taken to reduce account-takeover, unwanted-discovery, public-exposure and third-party-access risks.

## Accounts Audited

| Account | Account opened | Areas reviewed |
|---|---:|---|
| Facebook | 30 December 2010 | Profile visibility, post audience, friends list, sessions and connected apps |
| Instagram | 2017 | Tags, mentions, sharing, reuse and login security |
| Google | 2020 | Two-Step Verification, recovery methods, prompts and devices |
| LinkedIn | 2020 | Two-factor authentication, sessions, connections, discoverability and permitted services |

## Completed Hardening Actions

1. Enabled Google 2-Step Verification.
2. Restricted Instagram tag permissions.
3. Reduced Instagram sharing and content-reuse permissions.
4. Limited LinkedIn discoverability by phone number.
5. Disabled LinkedIn connection-list visibility.
6. Enabled LinkedIn two-factor authentication.
7. Ended old LinkedIn sessions, reducing the list from seven sessions to one current session.
8. Restricted Facebook profile and contact-information visibility.
9. Reviewed and reduced unnecessary Facebook connected-app access.

Additional checks included reviewing Meta login sessions, LinkedIn permitted services, recovery options and public search-engine exposure.
